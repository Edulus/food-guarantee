# A Food Guarantee for Canada

A policy white paper by Edward Yersh, BA, McGill University (November 2026).

It proposes the Three Layer Coordinated Price Guarantee: a minimum price for farmers who grow a short list of staple foods, a published maximum shelf price for a basic version of those foods at large grocery chains, and a monthly supplement for households whose incomes remain too low.
